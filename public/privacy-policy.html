Privacy Policy

RR PATEL OVERSEAL & EDUCATION PVT LTD
Effective Date: 08 January 2026

RR PATEL OVERSEAL & EDUCATION PVT LTD ("we", "our", or "us") operates an internal Customer Relationship Management (CRM) portal used by authorized employees and administrators. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect user information when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • User Account Information: User name and email address (via Google OAuth authentication)
  • Google Calendar Data: Calendar events and appointments (only when calendar features are enabled and authorized by the user)
  • CRM Data: Internal CRM usage data related to leads, tasks, appointments, and scheduling
  • Usage Data: Logs of application usage for security and audit purposes

2. Use of Google OAuth & APIs

Our application uses Google OAuth for secure authentication and Google Calendar APIs for calendar management features. We only access data that is strictly required for these features to function properly.

  • No Google user data is used for advertising or marketing purposes
  • No Google user data is shared with third parties
  • Access is limited to authorized internal users only
  • Google OAuth tokens are stored securely and can be revoked at any time
  • We comply with Google's API Services User Data Policy

3. How We Use Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Secure user authentication and access control
  • Calendar and appointment management
  • Internal workflow optimization and business operations
  • Security monitoring and audit logs
  • System maintenance and improvement

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share user data with external parties. Data access is limited to authorized internal administrators only, and only for legitimate business purposes. We may share data if required by law or to protect our legal rights.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect data from unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure. This includes:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS)
  • Secure storage of authentication tokens
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Access controls and user authentication

6. Data Retention

User data and OAuth tokens are stored securely and retained only as long as necessary for business and compliance purposes. OAuth permissions can be revoked at any time through Google Account settings or by contacting us. Upon account termination, data will be deleted in accordance with our data retention policy.

7. User Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request access to your personal data
  • Revocation: Revoke Google OAuth permissions at any time
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of account data (subject to company policy and legal requirements)
  • Portability: Request a copy of your data in a portable format

8. Cookies and Tracking

We use session cookies and authentication tokens to maintain your login session. These are essential for the application to function and are not used for tracking or advertising purposes.

9. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the application after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
